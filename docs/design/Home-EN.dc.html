Our approach

One team from junior to elite.

Riders develop in one structure: the same staff, the same standards for training and equipment, and a steadily higher level of racing.

U19
Juniors8 riders in 2026. Road, track and cyclocross; Polish and European junior championships.

Under-23s10 riders in 2026. The UCI calendar in Europe and Polish national titles.
Elite
Elite2 riders in 2026. Team leaders in 1.Pro and 2.Pro races.
Staff

A five-person staff, one plan.

Sport directors, a coach, a team manager, a mechanic and a doctor. Racing, training and recovery are planned in one place.

Club Pro

Part of the Ministry of Sport's elite programme.

The programme supports clubs with the best results in developing children and young athletes, as ranked by Poland's Youth Sport System.

UCI races

In 2026: Scheldeprijs, De Brabantse Pijl and Lotto Thüringen (1.Pro), and Tour de Pologne Women (2.Pro).
